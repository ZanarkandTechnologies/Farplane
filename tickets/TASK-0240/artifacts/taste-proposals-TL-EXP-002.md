# TL-EXP-002 Taste Proposals

## Context

Operator feedback rejected TL-EXP-001 because the concepts were shallow hook
cards, not proposals. TL-EXP-002 regenerates planning with the TasteProposal
shape so Kenji can judge the bet from Telegram before any thread is drafted.

## Proposal A: The Fake Intern Who Makes Shipping Physical

```text
title: The Fake Intern Who Makes Shipping Physical
one_line_bet: Pocket Intern wins if the thread makes a silly wind-up toy feel like a tiny ritual for turning vague work into shipped work.
audience_or_buyer: Founder/builders with chaotic desks, unfinished ideas, and one friend they always joke about needing to "ship already."
taste_insight: The product is funniest when the fake employee badge and "ship it" stamp are treated as a real workflow object, not just a novelty gag.
artifact_shape: 8-post X/Twitter thread with a dead-simple ritual: write the task, stamp it, send proof, celebrate the tiny physical click.
core_angle: Your smallest teammate is a toy that cannot think, cannot schedule, and still somehow makes shipping feel more official.
execution_beats:
  - Open with the desk ritual: "I hired an intern whose only skill is approving sticky notes."
  - Show the founder problem: too many tabs, half-finished ideas, no satisfying finish line.
  - Reveal Pocket Intern as a wind-up "employee" with a fake badge and stamp.
  - Turn the stamp into a rule: if it gets stamped, it ships today or gets crossed out.
  - Give three concrete uses: bug fix note, launch checklist, "send the scary email."
  - Make the joke tactile: the click of the stamp becomes a tiny release ritual.
  - Name the gift use: for the friend who has 47 ideas and needs one plastic coworker.
  - CTA: "Open role: Pocket Intern, under $40, zero equity."
why_it_could_win:
  - It makes the toy weirdly useful, which matches the scenario goal.
  - It gives the thread a concrete behavior instead of just a funny line.
  - The desk ritual is easy to imagine, photograph, and gift.
what_would_make_it_cringe:
  - Too much productivity-guru language would flatten the absurdity.
  - Founder in-jokes could make the product feel narrower than it is.
references_or_taste_pack: AGI Toy Shop scenario; Pocket Intern badge + stamp props; "funny and weirdly useful" buyer reaction.
feedback_question: Keep, revise, or reject this ritual angle?
next_if_approved: Draft the 8-post social thread with the stamp ritual as the spine and no external posting.
```

## Proposal B: A Performance Review For A Toy Employee

```text
title: A Performance Review For A Toy Employee
one_line_bet: Pocket Intern becomes memorable if the thread is written as a deadpan HR performance review of a wind-up employee.
audience_or_buyer: People who like fake corporate artifacts, founder jokes, and shareable product characters with just enough business nonsense.
taste_insight: The fake employee badge is the product's strongest storytelling object; the thread should make the toy feel like a character being evaluated.
artifact_shape: 7-post performance-review thread with headings like Strengths, Growth Areas, Compensation, Culture Fit, and Rehire Decision.
core_angle: This intern has one measurable KPI: stamping "ship it" on your desk notes with total confidence.
execution_beats:
  - Open: "I conducted a 30-day performance review for our new intern."
  - Role summary: wind-up office assistant, desk morale, stamp-based approvals.
  - Strengths: punctual when wound, concise feedback, strong bias toward shipping.
  - Growth areas: cannot use Slack, cannot attend standup, compensation model unclear.
  - Culture fit: excellent at reducing overthinking because it only says one thing.
  - Buyer bridge: gift it to the person who needs a fake coworker and a real nudge.
  - CTA: "Rehire decision: approved. Salary: under $40 and one sticky-note stack."
why_it_could_win:
  - The format lets every post carry both a joke and a product detail.
  - It creates a repeatable character without copying any real creator voice.
  - It is easy to skim on a phone because each section has a clear label.
what_would_make_it_cringe:
  - If the HR parody gets too elaborate, the product could disappear.
  - Too many startup terms could make it feel like LinkedIn satire instead of a gift.
references_or_taste_pack: AGI Toy Shop scenario; fake employee badge; deadpan product-character copy.
feedback_question: Keep, revise, or reject this performance-review format?
next_if_approved: Draft the sectioned performance-review thread and keep the product visible in every post.
```

## Proposal C: The Desk Toy That Bullies Your Ideas Into The World

```text
title: The Desk Toy That Bullies Your Ideas Into The World
one_line_bet: Pocket Intern can stand out if the thread frames it as a charmingly bossy desk object that turns hesitation into a tiny public commitment.
audience_or_buyer: Builders, students, and creators who love absurd objects that make creative work feel less lonely and more game-like.
taste_insight: The "ship it" stamp is emotionally useful because it externalizes permission; the toy gives people a dumb little ceremony for stopping revision loops.
artifact_shape: 8-post narrative thread moving from private hesitation to stamped commitment to showing a friend.
core_angle: Sometimes the best creative tool is a tiny wind-up coworker whose only opinion is "ship it."
execution_beats:
  - Open with the universal loop: rewrite, rename, re-plan, do not ship.
  - Introduce the absurd intervention: a toy intern with a stamp and badge.
  - Show the first use: stamping a sticky note before sending a half-scary draft.
  - Make it social: send a photo of the stamped note to a friend as proof.
  - Explain why it works: the ritual is silly enough to lower the stakes.
  - List playful use cases: class project, tiny launch, gift note, desk bet.
  - Bring back the product: Pocket Intern is under $40 and does not ask what AI model you use.
  - CTA: buy one for the friend whose ideas deserve a tiny stampede.
why_it_could_win:
  - It broadens beyond founders while keeping the builder audience.
  - It explains the emotional job of the product, not only the joke.
  - It has a concrete social sharing mechanic: stamped-note photo proof.
what_would_make_it_cringe:
  - The word "bullies" can feel too aggressive if not softened by charm.
  - It risks sounding like self-help unless the copy stays playful and specific.
references_or_taste_pack: AGI Toy Shop scenario; shareable gifts; weird internet objects; "funny and weirdly useful" goal.
feedback_question: Keep, revise, or reject this bossy-desk-ritual angle?
next_if_approved: Draft a broader-audience social thread with the stamped-note proof mechanic.
```

## Recommendation

Pick Proposal A if the priority is commercial clarity and a concrete buying
reason. Pick Proposal B if the priority is memorability and character. Pick
Proposal C if the priority is broader audience and emotional resonance.
