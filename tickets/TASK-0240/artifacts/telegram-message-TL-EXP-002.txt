Taste Loop TASK-0240: Pocket Intern social thread proposals

Why this matters: the last cards were too thin. These are fuller TasteProposals so you can judge the idea from Telegram before we draft anything.

A. The Fake Intern Who Makes Shipping Physical
Bet: make Pocket Intern feel like a tiny ritual for turning vague work into shipped work.
Audience: founders/builders with chaotic desks and unfinished ideas.
Taste insight: the badge + "ship it" stamp should act like a real desk workflow, not just a gag.
Artifact shape: 8-post thread built around a sticky-note shipping ritual.
Story: hire a toy intern -> name the half-finished-work problem -> reveal the stamp -> make "if it gets stamped, it ships today" the rule -> show bug fix / launch checklist / scary email uses -> gift CTA.
Why it could win: concrete, visual, weirdly useful.
Cringe risk: could become productivity-guru sludge if it loses the joke.
If approved: draft the 8-post ritual thread.

B. A Performance Review For A Toy Employee
Bet: make Pocket Intern memorable as a fake employee being reviewed like a real teammate.
Audience: people who like fake corporate artifacts, founder jokes, and product characters.
Taste insight: the fake employee badge is the strongest story object.
Artifact shape: 7-post HR performance-review thread: Strengths, Growth Areas, Compensation, Culture Fit, Rehire Decision.
Story: "30-day performance review" -> role summary -> strengths: stamps ship it -> growth areas: cannot Slack -> culture fit: reduces overthinking -> gift bridge -> under-$40 salary joke.
Why it could win: every post can be a joke and a product detail.
Cringe risk: could drift into stale LinkedIn satire if too corporate.
If approved: draft the sectioned performance-review thread.

C. The Desk Toy That Bullies Your Ideas Into The World
Bet: make Pocket Intern a charmingly bossy object that turns hesitation into public commitment.
Audience: builders, students, and creators who like absurd objects that make work less lonely.
Taste insight: the stamp externalizes permission; it gives people a dumb ceremony for ending revision loops.
Artifact shape: 8-post narrative from private hesitation to stamped-note photo proof.
Story: rewrite/replan loop -> toy intern intervention -> stamp a sticky before sending a scary draft -> text stamped proof to a friend -> explain why silly lowers stakes -> use cases -> gift CTA.
Why it could win: broader than founders and emotionally specific.
Cringe risk: "bullies" may feel harsh; needs playful handling.
If approved: draft the broader-audience proof-mechanic thread.
