# Cross-Format Transformation Audit

## Change

- Before: an approved exemplar unlocked controlled variants, but the contract
  did not require one ticket to transform the skeleton across formats.
- After: the same ticket first transforms the approved proof spine into the
  configured pack—primary video, short cuts, carousel, X, and LinkedIn—then
  produces controlled variants.
- Human approval still gates the skeleton and exemplar. Publication, filming,
  spend, external generation, outreach, and account mutation remain separate.

## Why

Once the creative skeleton is good, each format is a representation of the
same accepted idea, not a new planning problem. Keeping the pack together
prevents repetitive content tickets while preserving the human taste loop.

## Proof

- Planner contract binds source, audience, goal, and channels.
- Todo and expansion sections require transformation before variation.
- The skill still blocks production before skeleton approval and expansion
  before exemplar approval.
