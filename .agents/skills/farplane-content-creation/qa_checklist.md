# Farplane Content Creation QA

- [ ] The required turn receipt preserves one configured stable problem plus canonical system/feature refs and names phase, route, audience problem, visible result, goal, channel, accepted evidence, taste references, proof limits, authority, approved-plan state, and active gate; the hook does not lead with an internal feature name.
- [ ] Planning presents one Best Bet by default and at most three distinct proposals, then records human approval before execution.
- [ ] The frozen skeleton includes an approved plan ref, invariants, variable axes, proof spine, prohibited claims, and a clear planning-reopen rule.
- [ ] One exemplar is human-optimized before a same-ticket cross-format pack and the default ten-variant batch; transformations preserve the proof spine, and every variant declares changed axes, preserved invariants, expected learning, named QA results, rank, and comparative rationale.
- [ ] Feedback receipts persist signal, learning, next hypothesis, and promotion state; independent review supports the shortlist while publication, outreach, spend, filming, external generation, and account mutation remain separately gated.
