# Farplane Content Creation QA

- [ ] The required turn receipt names phase, route, audience, goal, channel, evidence or hypothesis, taste references, proof limits, authority, approved-plan state, and active gate.
- [ ] Planning presents one Best Bet by default and at most three distinct proposals, then records human approval before execution.
- [ ] The frozen skeleton includes an approved plan ref, invariants, variable axes, proof spine, prohibited claims, and a clear planning-reopen rule.
- [ ] One exemplar is human-optimized before the default ten-variant batch; every variant declares changed axes, preserved invariants, expected learning, named QA results, rank, and comparative rationale.
- [ ] Feedback receipts persist signal, learning, next hypothesis, and promotion state; independent review supports the shortlist while publication, outreach, spend, filming, external generation, and account mutation remain separately gated.
