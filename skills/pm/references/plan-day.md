# Plan Day (interactive)

Use when the operator asks to plan, negotiate, or replan today. This is a live
conversation, not an automation: propose, let the operator push back, then write.

```text
plan_day(Life Plan + Plan Week + carryover + today's capacity) -> negotiated Today list
```

## Inputs

- `Life Plan` page: game, guardrails, ranked plays, Great Work Check.
- Newest pinned `Plan Week`: this week's intentions and `Today` section.
- Yesterday's unfinished items and any new ideas or messages since.
- Today's capacity. Ask once if unknown; never assume hours or energy.

## Procedure

1. **Open with yesterday.** List what is done and what carried over. Starting
   from yesterday's work is the habit being built.
2. **Pick one main outcome** from this week's critical path. Prefer the task
   that could visibly fail or that has been avoided; do it first.
3. **Add at most three supporting items,** and at most six in total.
   - Include the daily unit: one deal action or one piece of content, taken
     from Deals or Studio work.
   - Include one small piece of fun.
4. **Shape the day for flow.** Group by kind of work: outreach and messages,
   building, filming or writing. Do not interleave them.
5. **Negotiate.** Every item the operator adds must name what it displaces.
   Run new ideas through the Life Plan triage first (fit, speed to cash,
   ownership, compounding, effort) and answer kill, park, or test.
6. **Commit.** Restate the final list in order. Only after the operator accepts,
   write it to the `Today` section of Plan Week and read it back.

## Rules

- Never send, publish, or spend as part of planning.
- Unclear ownership and new commitments stay proposals until accepted.
- A day with no deal action or no shipped piece is allowed, but say so.
- Close the day with three lines: done, not done and why, what carries over.
