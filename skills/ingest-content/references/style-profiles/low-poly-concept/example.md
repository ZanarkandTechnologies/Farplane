# Example: How a Queue Becomes a Bottleneck

```text
brief: Explain why one slow processing stage backs up an otherwise fast system.
audience: product and operations teams
method: explainer
profile: low-poly-concept
```

1. Establish three matte processing gates on one ground plane; blue tokens move
   through all three at equal intervals.
2. Turn the middle gate amber and slow its cycle while the camera remains fixed.
3. Show tokens grouping before the amber gate and empty space after it.
4. Lift to a restrained top-down view and label arrival rate versus service
   rate once the spatial pattern is visible.
5. Restore capacity; show the queue shrink and return to the original wide view.

QA: stable entity roles, one causal spatial transformation, orientation-safe
camera movement, no presenter dependence, and a legible system-level payoff.
