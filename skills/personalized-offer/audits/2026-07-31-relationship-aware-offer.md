# Relationship-Aware Offer Audit

## Before / After / Example

> **Before:** One fit map flowed directly into one recommended offer.
>
> **After:** The same public signature derives an honest approach frontier from
> existing proof, use-case roots, entities/project context, and supplied
> relationship context. It keeps commercial movement, relationship value, ask
> burden, and relationship risk separate, then selects one approach.
>
> **Example:** Maya's fixture contains two supported paths rather than inventing
> a third; the lower-burden sample contribution wins over a cold workflow-audit
> ask and alone reaches the unsent email.

## Preserved Gates

- No new public parameter, skill, entity field, compiler behavior, or numeric
  relationship-points total.
- No invented introduction, favor debt, resource, shared interest, private
  preference, or thoughtful gesture.
- Only stable facts, sourced cues, history, and open questions may enter a
  proposed entity Markdown delta.
- Option ranks, selection logic, and message prose remain report-local.
- Only the selected strategy reaches Copywriting Advisor; requested channels
  only; all drafts remain unsent.
- CRM, outreach, publishing, enrichment, and spend remain approval-gated.
