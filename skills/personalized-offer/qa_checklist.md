# Personalized Offer QA Checklist

Read before execution and apply again to the report, copy, and CRM delta. Ask an
independent reviewer to try to reject material customer-facing packages.

## Checks

1. **Qualified, respectful fit** — resolve one person, company context, accepted
   usecase/offer, and proof direction or route upstream. Connect professionally
   relevant public/supplied evidence through role → decision → testable problem
   → outcome → proof; keep confidence, falsifier, and correction ask visible.
   Biography theater, sensitive/private dossiering, and invented pain fail.
2. **Honest approach frontier** — derive only viable options from available
   proof, use-case roots, entity/project context, and supplied relationship
   context. Show exactly three only when three exist; never invent resources,
   introductions, favors, preferences, or decorative choices. Judge commercial
   movement, relationship value, ask burden, and relationship risk separately
   with ordinal evidence, no numeric total, then select one and explain losses.
   A thoughtful gesture is optional, supported, appropriate, and never quid pro quo.
3. **Restrained offer, proof, and copy** — one smallest engagement, deliverables,
   non-goals, assumptions, reason-now hypothesis, proof limits, and next action
   are explicit; no invented ROI/readiness/company result. Only the selected
   strategy reaches `copywriting-advisor`, and only requested unsent channels
   render.
4. **State and action hygiene** — the diff-shaped CRM delta keeps stable fields
   separate from Markdown-body context. Reports, speculative pain, option ranks,
   selection logic, relationship judgments, and message prose remain linked
   artifacts. Approved entity Markdown compiles through
   `farplane entities compile`; generated `entities.json` is never hand-edited.
   CRM writes, sends, publishing, proposals, account changes, private data,
   enrichment, and spend remain unapplied without exact approval.
5. **Actionable learning** — name the correction ask, evidence gaps, next owner,
   evidence that would change the offer, and any unresolved relationship risk.

## Reviewer Output

Return every failed check with `violation | deferral`, exact evidence, smallest
repair, and overall `pass | revise | block`.
