# Platform Review Research

Search each relevant surface with a family of explicit queries. Start broad,
then revise the query ledger as claims, communities, authors, and failure modes
emerge.

| Surface | Discovery queries | Deep-read behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review sites and marketplaces | `<product> reviews`, `<product> alternative`, `<product> pricing`, `<product> <failure>` with a tracked-domain allowlist | Read recent positive, negative, and middling reviews; group by source lineage, version, segment, and use case. |
| Reddit | `site:reddit.com <product>`, `<product> vs`, `<product> switched`, `<product> problem`, plus subreddit-specific terms | Open multiple relevant threads, read the post and substantive comment branches, search within them for price, setup, support, failure, migration, and named alternatives. |
| X | `<product>`, exact feature names, complaint terms, author handles, demo links, and quoted phrases | Inspect threads and replies, follow linked demos or repos, distinguish first-hand use from reposts and promotion, and trace recurring authors. |
| YouTube | `<product> review`, `<product> tutorial`, `<product> vs`, `<product> problems`, and workflow-specific terms | Inspect several relevant videos, transcript sections, chapters, descriptions, linked resources, and useful comments. Record upload date and product version. |
| Official sources | product + feature, docs, changelog, pricing, status, security, migration | Verify claimed capability and current availability against the vendor's maintained surface. |

Use the emerging hypothesis tree to choose the next query: capability claims
lead to official verification; pain themes lead to review/community searches;
switching claims lead to alternative and migration searches; author or demo
signals lead to their linked work. Stop a branch when corroborated, falsified,
or unlikely to affect the decision. Report unsearched branches caused by the
budget or unavailable access.
