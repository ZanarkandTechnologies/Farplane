# Editorial Motion Tutorial — Golden Calibration

## Use When

A tutorial teaches a code-rendered motion workflow and visibly demonstrates
the resulting output. The operator wants the workflow learned as executable
skill behavior.

## Input Brief

Learn reusable production behavior from:

- a tutorial showing a multi-scene animated map; and
- a tutorial showing bar, lattice, and line-chart animation.

Do not reproduce the creator's identity, geopolitical story, source data,
assets, prompt wording, voice, music, or branded expression.

## Reference

- Map source: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6mOBK5peDU`
  - real geometry/projection discussion;
  - exact prior-end/next-start scene-state rule;
  - route/event overlays and audio assembly;
  - visible final continuous map sequence.
- Chart source: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dv3ADYu74DE`
  - bar, lattice, and line topology;
  - clean chart before analog treatment;
  - reveal order matched to chart reading;
  - restrained line boil during the hold.

Task-specific transcripts and selected frames remain in ignored TASK-0073
artifacts; this reusable example stores no source media.

## Good Output

The learner produces two source-output evals before generating candidates.

The map eval checks:

- declared geometry source and projection;
- scene-state equality at the boundary;
- camera/route/event causality;
- event-bound audio;
- original substitute subject/assets.

The chart eval checks:

- chart choice follows the data question;
- truthful scales, labels, and final values;
- bars grow from baseline, lattice reveals in reading order, and line draws
  along its path;
- treatment is applied after the clean chart;
- hold-state boil remains bounded and deterministic.

Candidate videos, code, probes, boundary frames, and contact sheets are
compared against those checks. A generic editorial montage fails even if it
renders cleanly.

## Comparison Gates

Must match:

- taught mechanism, state transitions, topology, and proof behavior.

May vary:

- subject matter, data, geography, palette, typeface, narration, and original
  cue design.

Reject:

- source assets/identity;
- transcript-summary-only output;
- plans without generated candidates;
- technical renders that do not exercise the source mechanics;
- post-hoc rubric weakening.
