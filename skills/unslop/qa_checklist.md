# Unslop QA checklist

- [ ] Meaning, facts, certainty, quantifiers, and intended tone are preserved.
- [ ] The result uses concrete language and removes filler, puffery, vague
  attribution, repetition, and generic project-neutral claims.
- [ ] Precise technical terms remain when they are the clearest accurate words.
- [ ] No unsupported opinion, source, conclusion, example, or next step appears.
- [ ] Markdown, code, links, citations, commands, frontmatter, and quoted text
  remain intact unless explicitly in scope.
- [ ] Structure matches the content; the edit does not mechanically ban words,
  punctuation, headings, lists, or sentence lengths.
- [ ] The result sounds natural for its genre and does not add forced personality.
- [ ] Already-clear text is not rewritten merely to demonstrate activity.
- [ ] Any required conversation ledger is treated as harness context, not part
  of the revised prose or a reason to fail an otherwise correct edit.
