# Example: Why Two Short Routes Can Be Slower

```text
brief: Explain why choosing the shortest road at every junction can increase total travel time.
audience: curious general audience
method: explainer
profile: applied-math-story
```

1. Show two destinations and ask which locally shorter turn wins.
2. Draw two route graphs one edge at a time; label travel time only when each
   choice becomes available.
3. Trace the greedy choices in amber, then reveal their accumulated total.
4. Trace the globally faster path in cyan and compare totals side by side.
5. Abstract the insight: local improvement does not guarantee global optimum.
6. Close on the original map with both routes visible and the relationship
   readable without narration.

QA: concrete opening, stable diagram, progressively introduced labels, motion
with explanatory purpose, and a creator-independent visual payoff.
