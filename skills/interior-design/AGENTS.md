# Interior Design Maintenance

## Scope

- `SKILL.md`
- `references/*`
- `templates/*`
- `examples/*`
- `evals/evals.json`

## Boundaries

- `interior-design` owns room program, spatial composition, adjacency,
  circulation, furniture/material grammar, lighting, negative space, and
  blockout acceptance.
- `visual-design` owns interface aesthetics and screen-level visual systems.
- Scene/frontend modules own implementation after direction acceptance.

## Checks

- Keep the package within the declared 10/5/5 surface budget.
- Preserve a real positive example and human-feedback acceptance gate.
- Do not claim physical building-code or professional design certification.
