# Interior Design QA Checklist

Read before shaping the interior and apply again to the finished brief or
artifact. Record `pass`, `violation`, or `unknown` with a short evidence note.

- [ ] `evidence_integrity`: observed facts, operator statements, assumptions,
  and missing screenshots/files/references are separated; no unseen scene is
  described as audited.
- [ ] `program_and_scene_completeness`: occupants, activities, zones,
  adjacency, shell, camera, circulation, scale, occupied mass, negative space,
  materials, furniture grammar, lighting, and occlusion are addressed.
- [ ] `whole_room_options`: at least two materially different composition
  models exist, with one recommendation, tradeoff, and falsifiable blockout
  hypotheses rather than a palette or prop-only patch.
- [ ] `coherent_interior_language`: zones share an intentional architectural,
  furniture, material, scale, repetition, and lighting system while functional
  identity remains legible.
- [ ] `acceptance_separation`: a reviewable plan/blockout/annotated view exists;
  operator taste decides aesthetic acceptance, while navigation, collision,
  build, and accessibility checks remain separate guardrails.
