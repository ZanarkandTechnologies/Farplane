# Hatch Pet QA Checklist

Read before generation and apply again before packaging.

1. **Identity and consent boundary:** A real-person import has a user-supplied image; that image and explicit user notes outrank researched cues, and the result does not fabricate or expose private or sensitive information.
2. **Research boundary:** Person research is limited to public pet-relevant cues, sources are recorded, facts and inference are separated, and inaccessible sources are not guessed.
3. **Animation contract:** Ideas are mapped into the fixed nine Codex states; no added rows, altered state semantics, unreadable props, text, UI, scenery, or detached effects enter the atlas.
4. **Visual and deterministic proof:** `final/validation.json` and `qa/review.json` pass, the contact sheet and motion previews are inspected, identity/style remain stable, and transparent unused cells have no RGB residue.
5. **Package and evidence:** `pet.json` and `spritesheet.webp` are staged together, `qa/run-summary.json` names the source brief and profile URLs when used, and failed checks block packaging or identify the smallest repair scope.
