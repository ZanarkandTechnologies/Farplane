# X Thread QA Checklist

Apply this before returning a draft and before requesting review.

- [ ] The reader, promise, stage, voice constraint, and source mode are named.
- [ ] The hook works as a standalone feed post and makes a concrete promise.
- [ ] The actual tweet sequence is present; no option is only a premise or
  high-level angle.
- [ ] Each body tweet has one job and advances the story, lesson, teardown, or
  checklist.
- [ ] Claims, examples, anecdotes, and metrics are sourced or marked as a
  hypothesis; unsupported specificity was removed.
- [ ] The CTA fits the requested draft stage and does not imply permission to
  publish, schedule, comment, or reply.
- [ ] The artifact names media assumptions, character-budget risk, source gaps,
  and `publication_status: approval_required`.
