### Behind-the-scenes thread (after a shipped video or build)

Use this shape when the brief is "how I made it":

- `1/` names the hype the thread pushes back on and promises the honest version.
- Each following tweet is one production step with its own screenshot or clip
  and one line on what AI did and what it saved.
- One tweet shows something AI failed at.
- The last tweet gives a wishlist of what the tools still can't do, then the
  rule you work by.
