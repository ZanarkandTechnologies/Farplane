# Applied Math Story Prompts

## Required Variables

`topic`, `audience`, `concrete_problem`, `core_relationship`, `proof_or_source`,
`duration`, `aspect_ratio`, and `cta_or_payoff`.

## Story Prompt

Create a creator-neutral explainer for `{audience}` about `{topic}`. Begin with
`{concrete_problem}` and make the viewer predict an outcome. Build one stable
annotated diagram, revealing only the information needed for each beat. Derive
`{core_relationship}` from the concrete case, test it on a contrasting case,
and close on `{cta_or_payoff}`. Ground factual claims in `{proof_or_source}`.
Target `{duration}` at `{aspect_ratio}`.

## Visual Prompt

Use a restrained 2D instructional system: deep neutral background, readable
sans labels, thin construction lines, and one consistent accent color per
concept. Animate draw-on, trace, transformation, comparison, or zoom only when
it exposes a relationship. Keep equations subordinate to the diagram.

## Negative Prompt

No creator names, signatures, copied layouts, ornamental formulas, unrelated
particles, frantic camera motion, unexplained notation, or unsupported claims.
