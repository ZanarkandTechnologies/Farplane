# Late-90s Everyperson

This is the canonical recurring character for the Retro Low-Poly Consequence
profile: dark angular hair, long triangular nose, tired asymmetric eyes, teal
rolled-sleeve shirt, ochre trousers, dark belt, and blocky dark shoes.

The identity sheet owns identity, silhouette, wardrobe, and proportions only.
It is intentionally cleaner than the target footage. The profile and approved
scene grids—not this white-background card—own the dirty PS1 render surface.

## Immutable Anchors

- Human everyperson identity and long angular facial silhouette.
- Teal shirt, ochre trousers, dark belt, and dark block shoes.
- Gaunt proportions, stiff hands, and deliberately coarse polygon joints.
- Tired/deadpan expression family shown on the identity sheet.

## Allowed Variation

- Pose, camera angle, expression within the sheet, dirt, lighting, and
  per-polygon texture instability may change with the scene.
- A provider-safe variant may simplify facial detail only after review. It must
  retain the silhouette, hair, wardrobe, proportions, and palette closely
  enough that the operator recognizes the same character.

## Disallowed Substitutions

- Helmeted crash-test mannequin, robot, masked person, glowing-eye figure, or
  generic faceless pedestrian.
- Different hair silhouette, wardrobe palette, body proportions, or footwear.
- Any safety fallback that changes visible identity without a new storyboard
  review and explicit approval.

The existing helmeted motion board is a proof-only provider fallback. It is not
an approved production identity and must never silently replace this package.
