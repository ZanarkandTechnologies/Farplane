# Low-Poly Concept Prompts

## Required Variables

`topic`, `audience`, `entities`, `spatial_relationship`, `proof_or_source`,
`duration`, `aspect_ratio`, and `payoff`.

## Story Prompt

Create a creator-neutral low-poly explainer for `{audience}` about `{topic}`.
Represent `{entities}` as distinct simple objects with persistent color and
shape roles. Establish the full system, isolate `{spatial_relationship}`, show
the causal transformation, and return to a wide view where `{payoff}` is
visible. Ground factual claims in `{proof_or_source}`. Target `{duration}` at
`{aspect_ratio}`.

## Visual Prompt

Build a sparse low-poly 3D scene with matte materials, soft directional light,
clear silhouettes, and one ground plane. Use at most one restrained camera move
per beat. Communicate through placement, grouping, enclosure, scale, collision,
and flow; keep labels short and attached to stable entities.

## Negative Prompt

No creator names, copied scenes, branded characters, photoreal texture noise,
cinematic fly-throughs without explanatory value, unstable color roles, or
unsupported claims.
