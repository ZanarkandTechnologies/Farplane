# Low-Poly Concept

## Intent

Explain an invisible system through a small, legible 3D world whose objects,
distances, and transformations act as a spatial metaphor.

## Grammar

- Reduce each concept to one simple geometric entity with a stable color role.
- Stage objects on a sparse ground plane with soft directional light and clear
  depth separation.
- Use one restrained camera move per beat: orbit, dolly, top-down reveal, or
  push-in. Prefer object motion when the relationship itself changes.
- Show causality through placement, scale, collision, flow, grouping, or
  enclosure rather than explanatory decoration.
- Keep materials matte, silhouettes distinct, and labels minimal.
- Begin with the whole system, isolate one mechanism, then return to the whole
  system with the consequence visible.

## Negative Constraints

- No named-creator imitation, copied characters, exact source scenes, branded
  props, photoreal clutter, glossy asset-pack sameness, or purposeless camera
  fly-throughs.
- Reject a talking-head-only output; a presenter may narrate but cannot replace
  the spatial explanatory carrier.

## Provenance

Compiled from the operator's request for a reusable low-poly explainer family.
It records broad 3D communication principles and contains no copied source
media or creator-specific identity cues.

## QA Assertions

- Every recurring concept has a stable shape and color assignment.
- Spatial changes correspond to a claim in the script.
- Camera movement preserves orientation and reveals necessary information.
- The final wide view makes the system-level consequence legible.
