# Candidate 1: Explicit Engine Budget

Status: `carry_to_seedance`, not promoted.

Single hypothesis: replace broad “low-poly” quality language with visible
engine-budget constraints:

- raw low-budget 2001–2004 console gameplay/cutscene capture;
- aggressively simple flat-shaded meshes and stiff keyframed motion;
- 128–256 px nearest-neighbor texture atlases with repeated texel blocks;
- vertex lighting, one hard directional light, jagged edges, no anti-aliasing;
- no PBR, normal maps, realistic skin/materials, ambient occlusion, ray
  tracing, global illumination, bloom, depth of field, or cinematic grading.

Reference-layer proxy improved from 6/10 to 8/10. Seedance motion, mechanism
legibility, continuity, and full-video quality remain untested.
