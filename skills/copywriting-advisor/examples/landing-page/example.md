# Compact Landing Page Copy Packet Example

```text
Audience:
  solo service founders who are getting referrals but losing leads after the
  first call
Product:
  a lightweight follow-up CRM that writes the next message from call notes
Page job:
  make the founder feel the cost of delayed follow-up and try the product
One reader:
  a tired founder with five open conversations and no clean next step
Emotional job:
  turn "I am dropping balls" into "I know exactly who needs what next"
Product truth:
  the product turns notes into a short next-message queue
Promise:
  leave every sales call with tomorrow's follow-up already drafted
Reason to believe:
  imports call notes, extracts promises, drafts concise next steps, and marks
  stale conversations
Main objection:
  "I do not want another CRM to maintain"
Story spine:
  tension: good calls die in the gap after the call
  turn: the system turns the messy notes into next actions
  proof: each lead gets a drafted message and age/status signal
  action: start with the last five calls
Page copy:
  hero: Stop losing warm leads in the follow-up gap.
  subhead: Turn call notes into tomorrow's next messages before the day ends.
  sections:
    - Every promise has a place.
    - See the conversations going cold before they vanish.
    - Send the next message without rebuilding the whole story.
  CTA variants:
    - Draft my follow-ups
    - Clean up my open leads
    - Start with five calls
Word bank:
  warm lead, open loop, promised, next message, going cold, clean queue,
  follow-up gap, tomorrow's note
Cut list:
  - "revolutionize your pipeline"
  - "AI-powered sales productivity platform"
  - "unlock seamless client communication"
QA verdict:
  pass - 27 non-empty copy lines, one promise, concrete emotional situation,
  proof tied to product behavior, final public copy still needs founder review.
Next owner:
  landing-page for section architecture or seo-content-advisor for an article
  about referral follow-up.
```
