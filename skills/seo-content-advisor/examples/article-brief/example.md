# People-First SEO Article Brief Example

```text
Audience:
  solo consultants who lose warm leads after discovery calls
Product:
  a lightweight follow-up CRM that drafts next messages from call notes
Topic:
  how to follow up after a sales call
Search intent:
  practical how-to; reader wants a template and timing guidance
Reader promise:
  leave the article with a follow-up rhythm and three messages they can send
Original angle:
  the real failure is not laziness; it is losing the promise made during the
  call, so the workflow starts from call notes rather than a generic cadence
Proof/source set:
  product behavior: extracts promises, drafts next step, flags stale leads
  source need: current best-practice examples for email timing before publish
Title options:
  - How to Follow Up After a Sales Call Without Sounding Desperate
  - The Follow-Up Gap: Why Good Sales Calls Go Cold
  - A Simple Sales Call Follow-Up Workflow for Solo Consultants
Meta description:
  A practical follow-up workflow for solo consultants: what to send, when to
  send it, and how to turn call notes into clear next messages.
Outline:
  H1: How to Follow Up After a Sales Call Without Sounding Desperate
  sections:
    - Answer first: send the promised next step within 24 hours.
    - Why follow-up fails: the call promise disappears.
    - The three-part message: context, promised value, next action.
    - Templates for warm, delayed, and stalled leads.
    - A simple note-to-message workflow.
    - When to stop following up.
Internal links:
  - sales call notes template
  - lightweight CRM for consultants
FAQ candidates:
  - How soon should I follow up after a sales call?
  - What should I say if I forgot to follow up?
  - How many times should I follow up?
Freshness notes:
  Email timing guidance should be checked against current credible sources
  before publication.
Do-not-claim:
  Do not claim improved close rate without product/customer evidence.
SEO QA verdict:
  revise before publish - useful intent and original angle are clear, but
  timing best practices need current source verification and founder review.
Next owner:
  copywriting-advisor for landing-page hero/CTA language or review before
  public article publication.
```
