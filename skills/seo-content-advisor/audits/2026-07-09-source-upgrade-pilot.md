# Skill Audit

## Change

- Before: SEO Content Advisor required intent fit, people-first usefulness,
  originality, proof, and publication gates.
- After: SEO Content Advisor now requires an explicit `information gain` beyond
  generic ranking-page paraphrase.
- Why: Google people-first guidance and current SEO writing sources converge on
  helpfulness, original value, reader-first answers, and avoiding search-engine
  theater.
- Tradeoff accepted: Added one first-load field and one QA/eval guard rather
  than a large SEO-source reference.
