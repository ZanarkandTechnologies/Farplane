# TASK-0001: Find the first customer

## Summary

Find one real, reachable customer whose urgent problem matches the project's
intended value. Prefer direct evidence from a specific person over a broad
market persona or an invented example.

## Scope

- In: identify the candidate, capture evidence of the problem, record a safe
  contact path, and prepare the smallest useful discovery conversation.
- Out: bulk outreach, purchased lead lists, fabricated customer evidence,
  public posting, contractual commitments, and unapproved external contact.

## Done / Proof

```text
done_when:
  - one named customer or accountable buyer is recorded
  - the urgent problem and why this customer has it are supported by traceable evidence
  - a reachable contact path and the next discovery action are recorded
  - the operator approves the evidence and any external contact before the ticket closes
proof:
  - customer or entity reference
  - problem evidence and source refs
  - approved discovery prompt or conversation notes
```

## Program

Start with existing customer context, authorized research, and direct evidence.
Write the candidate, problem, evidence, contact path, and approval request into
this ticket or its artifacts. Stop before contacting anyone unless the operator
has approved that action.
