# Review Offer Golden Trace

## Input

A fractional COO offer produces strong retained-client outcomes, but prospects
describe its proposal as “expensive weekly meetings.” Market and delivery are
sound; the objective is better qualified-prospect comprehension.

## Decisive trace

1. Retention and outcomes support delivered value; prospect language shows perceived value is format-led.
2. Category-of-one and visible-value criteria are weak/partial; market, outcome, and delivery do not justify a repair-first branch.
3. The binding constraint is that prospects cannot see the installed operational outcome; bonuses, scarcity, price, and guarantee are downstream.
4. Before → After: “weekly COO calls” → “90-day operating-system installation with named decisions, milestones, and outcome recap.”
5. A value dashboard is one illustrative option, not required.
6. Show current and revised framing to five qualified prospects without changing price; compare outcome comprehension and fit objections.

## Accepted output

Recommend value/category framing first, preserve delivery and price, and use
the test result to decide whether to adopt the framing or investigate a deeper
fit objection. Generic advice would list ten tactics; this trace rejects nine
simultaneous changes because the observed constraint is specific.
