# Page Copy Method

Load after `SKILL.md` selects `page` and the source atoms exist (see
[source mining](source-mining-and-swipe-workflow.md)). It holds the message
map fields, the persuasion and lead choices, the copy-gap audit, and the copy
packet template.

## Message map

Name each field and the source atom behind it: one reader, awareness stage,
emotional job, product truth, dominant desire, market sophistication,
promised transformation, mechanism, reason to believe, main objection, and
one action.

## Story shape by awareness

| Awareness stage | Page story | Opens with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem-aware | Pain mirror | The reader's own words for the pain |
| Solution-aware | Mechanism | How this approach differs |
| Product-aware | Proof | Evidence, demo, testimonial |
| Most-aware | Offer and action | The deal and the button |

## Persuasion path

Pick one and say why it fits the reader stage. Use it as a diagnostic
structure, not a fill-in template.

- AIDA: cold attention.
- PAS: pain-aware urgency.
- 4Cs (clear, concise, compelling, credible): trust-sensitive clarity.
- FAB: feature-heavy products.
- ACC (awareness, comprehension, conversion): education to conversion.
- SLAP (stop, look, act, purchase): short urgency or offer moments.

Pick the lead posture separately: direct offer, promise, problem/solution,
secret, proclamation, or story, from what the reader already knows and how
skeptical the market is. Name the message-layer verdict (is the promise,
mechanism, or proof the weak layer) and the weakest conversion factor.

## Copy-gap audit (rewrites only)

Before drafting over existing copy, compare it against the source atoms for
missing needs, benefits, anxieties, proof, differentiation, and reader
language. Draft to close the named gaps.

## Section jobs

Hero: outcome or pain relief plus specificity. Subhead: mechanism plus reason
to believe. Pain: mirror source language without dramatizing. Mechanism: the
product move in plain words. Proof: evidence or concrete behaviour. Objection:
remove friction or name the tradeoff honestly. CTA: one action in the reader's
language. Every line carries desire, clarity, proof, objection handling, or
action; cut the rest.
