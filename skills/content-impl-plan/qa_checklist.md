# Content Impl Plan QA Checklist

- [ ] `brief-story-and-audience`: The plan resolves the ICP without invented
  demographics and names role/job, context, starting belief, friction,
  knowledge, emotional stake, objection, must-believe-after, desired action,
  one point of view, and stakes. Each causal beat records narration, visual
  direction, viewer question, state change, causal link, and `evidence |
  explanation | soul` function. Before asset search, every beat has distinct
  literal-evidence, causal-physical, and context/scale concepts with a selected
  concept and rejected rationales. Measured scenes normally span 4-5 seconds
  (documented 3.5-6 second exceptions), own one state change and causal handoff,
  and contain two or three story-bearing reveals. Opening, mechanism, and
  payoff thumbnail alternatives proceed through board, measured animatic,
  anchor frames, and asset lock. Silent/mute tests must preserve causal
  comprehension, clause alignment, eye direction, readable holds, and material
  identity.

- [ ] `asset-evidence-and-scene-readiness`: Every selected asset has one
  `prove | orient | explain | humanize | atmosphere | bridge` job, claim or
  `none`, evidence level, identity, motion purpose, provenance, rights, and
  acceptance check; metaphor, decoration, and wallpaper never prove facts.
  Each production scene has one manifest row with shared background, dominant
  main-topic asset, separate foreground asset, layer/reveal order, source or
  generation packet, owner, rights, output, accepted file, acceptance check,
  and exactly one readiness state: `storyboard_draft_ready`,
  `asset_packet_ready`, or `creative_lock_passed`. Missing visuals require an
  Asset Advisor discovery receipt and resolve through source, inspired
  generation with transferable/must-not-copy traits, or evidenced original
  generation. Charts and supplied PNGs fill only the main-topic slot. Planned
  filenames, cards, panels, labels, generic mocks/silhouettes, and newly drawn
  SVG/JSX substitutes are not genuine assets. Remotion stays blocked until
  accepted background/topic/foreground files and a representative layered
  frame pass.

- [ ] `visual-variance-and-treatment`: Main and foreground assets carry
  perceptual `asset_family_id` values; renames, crops, recolors, flips, and
  small rotations remain one family. Excluding shared backgrounds and declared
  transformed motifs, adjacent overlap is zero, with calibrated production
  bands of >=80% unique main and >=85% unique foreground families. Motifs name
  causal function and visible transformation while varying two other visual
  dimensions. Foreground receipts measure rendered nontransparent coverage,
  tight alpha bounds, edge contact, occlusion, and semantic relevance; roughly
  20-45% lower-frame mass or equivalent edge-spanning depth is calibration,
  never a canvas-size shortcut. Editorial/newsprint work uses a quiet stage,
  concrete narration-specific subjects, related foregrounds, rights-cleared
  raster texture in page/object coordinates, crisp subject alpha, inspectable
  final-resolution halftone scale, restrained registration, full-frame and
  close-crop proof, and independent style review. Global noise, tiny swatches,
  sepia, stains, tears, splatters, and scrapbook decoration cannot pass unless
  the story explicitly requires archival damage.

- [ ] `creative-inputs-and-realization`: The plan accepts `brand_kit?` and
  `tasty_pack?`, never aliases a `style_profile`, and gives approved Brand Kit
  identity/policy precedence. Every considered element is visibly chosen,
  augmented, rejected, conflicting, or unused with provenance and rationale;
  every selected element preserves `description`, `whyItWorks`, one resolved
  `goldenExample`, and one `goldenRecipe`, then maps to a beat, artifact,
  advisor action, cue, edit, or production rule. Only the six canonical
  Creative Element kinds are used. Selected editing elements route through
  Editing Advisor before rendering and become an addressed ordered recipe.
  Before spend, the plan exposes its hypothesis, conflicts, leverage map,
  low-fi demo, and actual storyboard images/notes. Child realization packets
  contain provenance, description, why, example, recipe, planned use, and
  acceptance. Repairs repopulate the authoritative packet rather than emitting
  a schema; supplied-chart/image fast tracks still emit concrete background
  and foreground packets.

- [ ] `ownership-review-and-terminal-proof`: A timing master (`voiceover |
  music | source_video | none`) is selected before final generation and orders
  measurement, storyboard/cue revision, asset work, and Remotion. Content Impl
  Plan orchestrates sibling Storyboard, Asset Advisor, Editing Advisor,
  Remotion, and Review/QA actions; Asset Advisor alone selects image, video,
  avatar, or audio realization children. Each action has inputs, outputs,
  acceptance checks, blockers, and topology obligations; provider spend waits
  for storyboard approval. Two to four ICP-derived decision lenses record
  objections, required proof, rejection reasons, and independent review before
  creative lock and again on the final artifact. Mechanical timing, reveal,
  family, geometry, and treatment receipts remain separate from scene-cited
  judgment of specificity, causality, hierarchy/depth, neighbor novelty,
  silent comprehension, and evidence integrity; hard blockers never average
  away. The terminal path records rights/likeness/music/source/brand/platform
  risks, real timing media and cues, realization receipts, captions,
  transitions, mix, Remotion proof, and review/QA evidence. With enough input,
  compile the strongest useful plan before isolating exact unresolved blockers
  instead of returning a generic request for context.
