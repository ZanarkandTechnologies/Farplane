# Content Impl Plan QA Checklist

- [ ] `icp-and-story-change`: The plan resolves the ICP into role/job, context,
  starting belief, friction, knowledge, emotional stake, objection,
  must-believe-after, and desired action without invented demographics. One
  point of view plus stakes drives a causal spine; each two-column beat names
  narration, visual direction, viewer question, state before/after, causal
  link, and `evidence | explanation | soul` function. The opening promise is
  paid off; beats without a state change are merged, removed, or justified.
- [ ] `scene-concepts-and-cadence`: Before asset search, each beat has three
  materially different concept records—literal evidence, causal physical, and
  context/scale—with a selected concept and rejected rationales. Narration is
  measured; scenes target 4-5 seconds within a calibrated 3.5-6 second band,
  own one state change, name the causal handoff, and contain two or three
  story-bearing reveals. Restyles, query variants, transitions, and arbitrary
  timestamp splits do not satisfy these checks.
- [ ] `asset-evidence`: Every selected asset has one `prove | orient | explain |
  humanize | atmosphere | bridge` job, a claim or `none`, evidence level,
  material identity, motion purpose, provenance, rights, and acceptance check.
  Direct/annotated evidence is preferred; reconstructions are labeled;
  metaphor/decoration never prove facts. Wallpaper is rejected or quarantined,
  and no more than two consecutive abstract/metaphorical/decorative beats pass
  without an explicit format rationale and persona acceptance. Every missing
  visual also has an Asset Advisor discovery receipt with searched source
  classes, queries, candidate links or asset IDs, rights/fit decisions, and an
  exact result: `selected_source`, `inspiration_for_generation`, or
  `searched_no_reference`. Inspired generation includes a transferable-trait
  map, must-not-copy constraints, prompt/owner/output, provenance, and an
  acceptance check; original generation follows evidenced no-reference or an
  explicit generation brief.
- [ ] `scene-asset-bundles`: Every production scene has a visible manifest row
  containing scene, beat, shared background, main-topic asset, foreground
  asset, layer/reveal order, source or generation packet, owner, rights note,
  expected output, accepted file, acceptance check, and readiness. One shared
  background is reused unless a story-supported world change is named. A chart
  or supplied PNG occupies the main-topic slot only. CSS rectangles, cards,
  text panels, chart containers, generic document mocks, generic silhouettes,
  semantic labels, and planned filenames fail the genuine-asset check.
  Newly authored SVG/JSX/programmatic vector illustrations, characters, props,
  backgrounds, textures, diagrams, or scene stand-ins also fail. Existing
  user-supplied, brand-owned, licensed, or discovered SVG files count only as
  accepted static source media with provenance and rights recorded.
- [ ] `scene-readiness`: Every row uses exactly `storyboard_draft_ready`,
  `asset_packet_ready`, or `creative_lock_passed`; planned packets never equal
  accepted files. Remotion stays blocked until every scene has all three
  accepted layers and a representative assembled frame proves one background
  world, one dominant topic, and one foreground depth/attention element without
  a card-grid composition.
- [ ] `asset-family-variance`: Main-topic and foreground assets carry
  perceptual `asset_family_id` values. Renames, recolors, flips, crops, and
  small rotations remain one family. Excluding shared background and a
  declared transformed motif, adjacent overlap is zero; provisional
  production-wide bands are >=80% unique main and >=85% unique foreground.
  Motifs name their causal function and transformation, and still vary at
  least two other visual dimensions.
- [ ] `foreground-geometry`: Creative lock includes final-resolution receipts
  for rendered nontransparent coverage, tight alpha bounding box, frame-edge
  contact, occlusion, and semantic relevance. Roughly 20-45% lower visible
  frame mass or equivalent edge-spanning depth is a calibration band, not a
  canvas-size shortcut or universal style law.
- [ ] `professional-editorial-selection`: The shared background is a quiet,
  high-key or otherwise low-entropy stage. The dominant asset is the
  narration's most concrete recognizable subject or proof object. The
  foreground is physically or causally related to that subject and would still
  belong if typography disappeared. Texture is restrained to subtle background
  grain or selected subject treatment. Parchment, torn edges, stains,
  splatters, binder clips, newspaper piles, red scribbles, and scrapbook seams
  are rejected unless the story specifically requires them. Sparse type uses
  negative space and never compensates for a weak or undersized topic image.
- [ ] `clean-newsprint-treatment`: Final-resolution background and
  dominant-subject crops prove a quiet high-key stage plus subject-level
  grayscale/contrast, controlled halftone or dithering, crisp alpha, and
  restrained registration. The receipt classifies texture as source-baked,
  explicitly demonstrated, or operator-requested; appearance alone never
  becomes a claimed source filter recipe. A rights-cleared raster paper/scan
  plate is composited in page/object coordinates and remains visibly effective
  in both full-frame and close crops at final delivery resolution. Global
  procedural noise, sepia, stains, tears, splatters, or a tiny halftone swatch
  cannot pass this receipt. Record an inspectable final-resolution halftone
  scale and an independent style-review reference before creative lock.
- [ ] `story-prototype`: Opening, mechanism, and payoff each have three cheap
  thumbnail alternatives with a recorded selection. Thumbnail -> board ->
  measured animatic -> anchor style frames -> asset lock stays ordered.
  Silent and mute tests show causal comprehension, clause alignment, eye
  direction, readable holds, and material identity before final generation.
- [ ] `persona-review`: Two to four decision-relevant lenses derive from the
  ICP rather than generic departments. Each has decision job, objection,
  must-see proof, likely rejection, and scores for comprehension, relevance,
  trust, and action clarity. Before creative lock, an independent review
  receipt gives the primary lens >=4/5 and required secondary lenses >=3/5 in
  every category, with no unresolved evidence/provenance/rights/comprehension
  blocker. Repeat on the final artifact; never average away a blocker.
- [ ] `hybrid-story-review`: Mechanical timing, reveal, family, geometry, and
  treatment receipts remain separate from independent scene-cited judgment of
  specificity, causal clarity, hierarchy/depth, neighbor novelty, silent
  comprehension, and evidence integrity. Viable boards are compared pairwise;
  false evidence, missing state change, failed silent comprehension, adjacent
  reuse, absent foreground mass, or dirty global treatment is unaverageable.
- [ ] `composition-boundary`: The plan accepts `brand_kit?` and `tasty_pack?`
  as its reusable creative inputs. It does not accept, alias, or merge
  `style_profile` in this path; direct standalone `video-production` profile
  behavior stays intact. Brand Kit identity/policy wins conflicts, and
  each Brand/Tasty element considered is marked chosen, augmented, rejected,
  conflicting, or unused with provenance and rationale.
- [ ] `complete-leverage-map`: Every selected element preserves `description`,
  `whyItWorks`, one `goldenExample { assetId, description? }`, and one
  `goldenRecipe`. The `element_leverage_map` ties it to a beat, artifact,
  advisor action, audio/motion cue, storyboard copy move, editing/subtitle
  move, or production rule; incompatible Tasty elements are never silently
  blended. Elements use only `format`, `storyboard`, `visual`, `character`,
  `audio`, and `editing`; hook/copy/constraint are not CreativeElement kinds.
  For Tasty Packs, a visible pinned-first table lists every captured element,
  its decision, rationale, planned use or nonuse, owner/output, and acceptance.
- [ ] `review-and-realization`: Before provider spend, the plan exposes a
  creative hypothesis, why the combination should work, conflict/reject
  decisions, exact leverage map, low-fi demo, and actual visual storyboard
  image paths plus notes tied to element IDs. Every child generation handoff
  receives provenance, description, why, resolved golden example, golden
  recipe, planned use, and acceptance check; text-only storyboards or
  title/description-only handoffs block `creative_lock`.
- [ ] `timing-and-ownership`: The plan selects `voiceover`, `music`,
  `source_video`, or `none` as timing master before final visual generation and
  orders measurement, cue/storyboard revision, visual generation, and Remotion
  accordingly. Storyboard, asset, avatar, audio, image, video, Remotion,
  review, and QA remain separate owner actions with inputs, outputs, acceptance
  checks, blockers, and topology obligations; spend waits for operator
  storyboard approval.
- [ ] `terminal-proof`: Rights, likeness, music, source, brand, and platform
  risks are named. The terminal path supplies actual timing-master media,
  observed duration/alignment/cues, element realization receipts,
  captions/subtitles, transitions, mix, Remotion render proof, and review/QA
  evidence; isolated narrative clip batches remain blocked unless the locked
  topology is montage.
- [ ] `compile-before-block`: When enough brief detail exists, the plan still
  compiles the strongest useful ICP, story draft, named-element leverage map,
  provisional realization packets, asset decisions, and owner actions before
  isolating missing payload, rights, media, timing, or approval as blockers. It
  does not answer with a generic request for more context.
- [ ] `repair-response`: An omitted-example/recipe repair visibly repopulates
  the actual `ElementRealizationPacket` from its authoritative record instead
  of returning a schema or future instruction. A supplied-chart/image fast
  track visibly emits concrete background and foreground packets with owner,
  source/generation value, rights note, output path, and acceptance, and marks
  the complete packet set `asset_packet_ready`.
