# Review Model Golden Trace

## Input

A course has rising refunds, low completion, and repeated reports that the
promised outcome is not achieved. The operator asks for a cheap front end and
upsell ladder.

## Decisive trace

1. Customer outcome and trust are materially broken.
2. The viability gate routes to offer/product repair; it does not optimize the ten money-model criteria as though cash sequencing were primary.
3. The binding constraint is that the core promise is not reliably delivered.
4. Before → After: refund-heavy, low-completion delivery → a bounded cohort reaches a defined outcome with acceptable completion and complaint signals.
5. Onboarding repair or a narrower promise may be tested; neither is mandatory without root-cause evidence.
6. Observe a small cohort's failure points and test one delivery correction before attraction, upsell, or continuity mechanisms.

## Accepted output

Return `offer/product repair`, the outcome-centered Before/After, and the
evidence-first experiment. Generic money-model advice would add monetization
layers that amplify harm; this skill stops at the prerequisite gate.
